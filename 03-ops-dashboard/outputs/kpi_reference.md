## Findings (verified in this run)

- **1. Phone staffing gap (from week 14).** Phone wait 49s -> 100s (+51s). CES 5.09 -> 3.98 (diff -1.11, 95% CI +/-0.11, p=2.3e-83); CSAT 4.18 -> 3.37 (diff -0.81, p=2.3e-87).
- **2. Email backlog (from week 23).** Email first-response SLA 80.6% -> 23.9% (diff -56.7%, p=1.1e-296); email contacts still open 253 of 1,079 after week 23 vs 29 of 4,843 before; oldest open email contact 46 days.
- **3. Channel resolution gap.** FCR by channel: Email 70.1%, Live Chat 70.1%, Web Form 65.9%, Social Media 65.3%, Phone 51.8%.
- **4. Billing volume spike without an experience change.** Billing cases/week 153 -> 302 during weeks 18-19, while Billing CSAT stayed 4.01 vs 4.11 (diff -0.10). Volume is not the same signal as experience.
- **5. Worst queue by satisfaction.** Complaints & Escalations CSAT 3.29 / NPS -35, Delivery & Logistics CSAT 3.97 / NPS +10, Technical Support CSAT 4.01 / NPS +13, Billing CSAT 4.10 / NPS +18, Account Management CSAT 4.22 / NPS +27.
- **Supporting drivers.** corr(log wait, CSAT) = -0.49 and corr(log wait, CES) = -0.48 for real-time channels (queue wait is only comparable there); corr(wait z-score within channel, CSAT) = -0.45. CSAT 3.14 when escalated vs 4.10 when not (0.97 lower); CSAT 4.26 when resolved first contact vs 3.41 when not.
